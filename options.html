Chrome zoom for MacOS — Settings

Zoom hotkey — hold it and scroll

How sensitivity works

Each step moves exactly one of Chrome's own zoom levels — the same levels Cmd+ and Cmd- cycle through (… 90% → 100% → 110% → 125% → 150% …). There is no smooth/continuous zoom.

A scroll wheel fires many events (a trackpad flick can fire dozens, with momentum after your fingers lift). To stop one flick zooming many levels deep:

  • Scroll deltas must add up to a threshold (≈ half a mouse notch) before a step fires — one notch = one step.
  • After each step there's a 140 ms cooldown where further scrolling is swallowed, so momentum dies instead of stacking steps.
  • The partial scroll amount resets after 100 ms of quiet, so slow separate ticks never combine into a surprise zoom.

Notes

  • Hold only the chosen hotkey — holding a second modifier (e.g. Cmd+Control together) won't trigger zoom, to avoid clashing with other shortcuts.
  • Shift may be held or not; it doesn't affect zooming.
  • Zoom is per-tab and uses Chrome's built-in zoom, so the % shown in Chrome's menu matches, and pages remember their zoom the usual Chrome way.
  • Chrome blocks all extensions on chrome:// pages, the New Tab page's built-in content, and the Chrome Web Store — scroll zoom can't work there. Every regular website works.
  • If you pick Control, trackpad pinch-to-zoom gestures (which Chrome reports as Control+scroll) will also step the zoom — consistent with Windows behaviour.
